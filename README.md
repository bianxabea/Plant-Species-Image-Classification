# Plant-Species-Image-Classification

---

## A. Project Review

### 📄 Brief description of the project

The project focuses on the classification of **herbal plants** through image recognition technology, specifically emphasizing the collection and preparation of a comprehensive dataset consisting of different varieties of medicinal herbs. The primary objective of this study is to gather high-quality images of selected herbal plants such as Butterfly Pea (Clitoria ternatea), Katakataka (Bryophyllum pinnatum), Lemon Verbena (Aloysia citrodora), Sage (Salvia officinalis), Roselle (Hibiscus sabdariffa) and many more.

### 🎯 Purpose of the image classification model

The purpose of the image classification model is to automatically identify and classify different herbal plants based on their visual features such as leaf shape, color, and texture. It uses a trained dataset to accurately recognize selected medicinal herbs, making plant identification faster and more reliable for educational and research purposes.
